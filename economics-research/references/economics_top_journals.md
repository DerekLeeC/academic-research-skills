## Economics Paper Formatting Conventions

### General Rules
1. **Citation style**: Chicago Author-Date (most common in economics)
   - In-text: (Author Year) or Author (Year)
   - Multiple authors: (Author1 and Author2 Year) or (Author1 et al. Year)
   - Reference list: alphabetical by first author surname

2. **Abstract**: Typically 100-150 words (shorter than other social sciences)

3. **JEL codes**: Required. Include 2-4 codes from the JEL classification system.
   - Format: Letter + two digits (e.g., D12, J24, L13)
   - [Full JEL classification](https://www.aeaweb.org/econlit/jelCodes.php)

4. **Keywords**: 3-5 keywords, typically placed below abstract with JEL codes

5. **Structure**: Introduction → (Model/Theory) → Data → Empirical Strategy → Results → (Robustness) → Conclusion
   - No "Literature Review" section in most top journals — integrate into Introduction
   - "Related Literature" paragraph(s) at the end of Introduction is standard

6. **Tables**:
   - Standard errors in parentheses below coefficients
   - Significance stars: * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01
   - Always report N, R², and clustering level
   - Column headers for different specifications
   - Table notes explain all variables and specifications

7. **Figures**:
   - Clean, grayscale-friendly (many journals print in B&W)
   - Include confidence intervals where applicable
   - Axis labels clear and readable
   - Event study plots: include vertical line at treatment date

8. **Footnotes**: Used for supplementary information; numbered sequentially

9. **Online appendix**: Common for robustness checks, additional tables, proofs

10. **Data & code**: Replication package expected (AER mandatory since 2005)

### LaTeX Template (Economics Standard)

```latex
\documentclass[12pt]{article}
\usepackage[margin=1in]{geometry}
\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}
\usepackage{natbib}
\bibliographystyle{chicago}  % or aer, ecta
\usepackage{booktabs}        % for professional tables
\usepackage{graphicx}
\usepackage{hyperref}
\usepackage{setspace}
\doublespacing

\title{Title Here}
\author{Author Name\thanks{Affiliation. Email. Acknowledgments.}}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
Abstract text (100-150 words).\\
\textbf{JEL Codes}: XXX, YYY, ZZZ\\
\textbf{Keywords}: keyword1, keyword2, keyword3
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\section{Data}
\section{Empirical Strategy}
\section{Results}
\section{Robustness}
\section{Conclusion}

\bibliography{references}
\end{document}
```
